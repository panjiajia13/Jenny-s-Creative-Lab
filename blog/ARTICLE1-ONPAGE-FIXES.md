## 🔴 P0 · 标签层（10 分钟，收益最大，先做）

### 1. Meta title —— 现在有两个硬伤
- **Before**：`10 Best Indie Alternative Music Bands`
- **问题**：① 写"10"但你其实是 11（不一致）；② 不含精确主词 `alternative indie bands`（被 On Page Checker 反复标红"title 不含关键词"）。
- **After**：
  ```
  11 Best Alternative Indie Bands & Artists to Hear in 2026
  ```
  → 精确含主词 `alternative indie bands` + 快赢词的 `artists`，修了 10→11，56 字符达标。

### 2. H1（文章标题）—— 精确词组被"Music"打散
- **Before**：`11 Best Alternative Indie Music Bands You Need to Hear Right Now (2026)`
- **问题**：`alternative indie … bands` 中间被 Music 隔开，精确信号弱（这就是它卡 pos 30、却在 `modern alternative indie artists` 精确 H2 上排 pos 6 的原因）。
- **After**：
  ```
  11 Best Alternative Indie Bands You Need to Hear Right Now (2026)
  ```
  → 去掉"Music"，`Alternative Indie Bands` 连续精确。**URL/slug 不动**（它在排名）。

### 3. Meta description —— 不含主词
- **Before**：`Discover the best indie alternative music bands, from classic indie rock artists to modern indie pop bands. Updated list with 10+ recommendations.`
- **After**：
  ```
  The best alternative indie bands and indie alternative artists to hear in 2026 — Arctic Monkeys, Wet Leg, Fontaines D.C., Mitski, Boygenius and more, sorted by mood.
  ```
  → 同时含两个目标词 + 具体乐队实体（提高点击率）+ 年份新鲜度。

## 🟠 P1 · 正文精修（半小时，符合"不堆砌"原则）

### 4. 反向操作：`alternative indie` 用得**太多**了，要**稀释**
On Page Checker 对头词 `alternative indie` 报的是 **"repeated too many times, use less often"**——这是过度优化信号。正好借机把重复的 `alternative indie music` 换成**变体目标词 + 具体乐队**，一举两得（既去重、又自然种下 `alternative indie bands` / `indie alternative artists`）。
- **开头段 [00] Before**：`…looking for something newer that doesn't quite have a name yet — this is my list of the 11 best **alternative indie music bands** worth your time in 2026.`
- **After**：`…or looking for the kind of **indie alternative artists** that don't quite have a name yet — this is my list of the 11 best **alternative indie bands** worth your time in 2026.`
  → 开头 100 词内出现精确 `alternative indie bands` + `indie alternative artists`，同时少了一次"music"重复。

### 5.「What Is Alternative Indie Music」段 —— 抢精选摘要（这词 SF=7，有摘要位）
GSC 显示 `what is alternative indie music`(pos 40)、`is alternative and indie the same`(pos 32) 有曝光但排名低。在该 H2 正下方**加一段可提取的直接回答**（同时补上 On Page Checker 要的语义词 `post punk` / `alternative rock bands` / `Talking Heads` / `greatest alternative`）：
- **新增段（放在 [02] 标题下、[03] 之前）**：
  ```
  So — is alternative and indie the same thing? Not quite, but the line has blurred almost to nothing. Alternative indie music is guitar-forward, independent-minded music that values feeling over formula: "indie" points to the DIY spirit, "alternative" to the broader rock lineage it grew from — post-punk, and the classic alternative of Talking Heads and R.E.M., down to today's alternative rock bands. Most of the greatest alternative artists now live comfortably on both sides of that line.
  ```
  → 直答问题（利于 featured snippet / AI 引用）+ 自然补 4 个语义词 + 加了约 70 词。

### 6. 每个乐队加一行**可扫读标签**（治"可读性差"+ 加词数 + 加关键词）
On Page Checker 三次报"readability difficult"。你的文风有深度不能砍，但可以**加结构**：给 9 个乐队每个末尾补一行统一格式（不动原有段落）：
```
**Start with:** [已有的那首歌/专辑] · **For fans of:** [已有的相似艺人]
```
你正文里其实已经有这些信息（散在句子里），把它固化成一行——**读者更好扫、H 结构更清晰、词数也上去了**。
> 进阶（可选）：把 9 个乐队名从"加粗段落"升级成 **H3 小标题**。你已经验证过"精确 H2 就能进首页"，多几个语义化 H3 对结构和排名都有利。

### 7. 补一次 `alternative music artists` 到正文
On Page Checker：body 不含此词。在「Modern」段开头 [16] 自然加一句即可：
- **After [16]**：`These are the modern **alternative indie artists** — and a couple of the most exciting **alternative music artists** full stop — whose audiences are still catching up…`
