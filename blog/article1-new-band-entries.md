## ① 放入「The Most Popular Alternative Indie Bands — Start Here」段（作为第 6 个，接在 Mitski 之后或 Arctic Monkeys 之后）

**The 1975** are the band people love to argue about, which is usually the surest sign a band matters. Matty Healy writes pop songs too self-aware to be simple and too sincere to be ironic — saxophone breaks, spoken-word confessions, choruses engineered to wreck you by the third listen. **Being Funny in a Foreign Language** stripped the maximalism back to something warmer and, somehow, made them better at it; "About You" quietly turned into the song a whole generation now files under *being seventeen*. Start with [Being Funny in a Foreign Language (2022)](https://open.spotify.com/album/6dVCpQ7oGJD1oYs2fv1t5M), then wander back through the sprawl. If they click, Wallows and Bleachers scratch the same over-produced, heart-on-sleeve itch.

## ② 放入「Modern Alternative Indie Artists Breaking Through」段（作为第 5 个，接在 Noah Kahan 之后）

**Soccer Mommy** — Sophie Allison — writes the kind of diaristic, guitar-forward songs that sound offhand until a single line stops you cold. There's a plainspoken honesty to her writing that never tips into self-pity: she just tells you the truth and lets the melody carry the weight. **Clean** still sounds like a debut with nothing to prove and everything to say — "Your Dog" opens with one of the great kiss-off lines in modern indie. Start with [Clean (2018)](https://open.spotify.com/album/36NLDBi2kX7XRHnyLzLOS8), then follow the thread into color theory (2020). If you love Phoebe Bridgers but want a little more bite, this is your next favourite artist.
